# Invoice Reminder

**For:** hardware shops and suppliers who sell on credit to contractors, schools, churches and fundis.

## What it does

Every morning at 7:30, it looks through your invoice sheet and finds every customer who has not paid on time. For each one, it writes a polite reminder that is ready to send. Each reminder comes in two versions: an email, and a short SMS/WhatsApp message.

The reminders get firmer the longer a customer goes without paying:

| How late | What the customer gets |
|---|---|
| 1 to 7 days | A friendly reminder |
| 8 to 30 days | A second reminder that offers to agree a payment plan |
| More than 30 days | A final reminder asking for payment within 7 days |

Every reminder already includes the invoice number, what was supplied, how much is still owed, and your Paybill and bank details.

It also makes a **chase list**: one page showing who owes you, how much, and for how long, with the oldest debts at the top.

## What you do each day

1. Open the **Outbox** folder.
2. Send each reminder by email, or copy its short message into WhatsApp.
3. Move the reminders you have sent into **Outbox\Sent**.

**Keeping the sheet up to date:** when a customer pays, write **yes** in the Paid column. If they pay part of it, write the amount in **Amount Paid**.

## What you never have to worry about

- **No customer is reminded twice for the same thing.** The next reminder is only written when the invoice moves to a firmer stage, or when its balance changes.
- **No reminder goes to someone who has paid.** If a customer pays before you send their reminder, the tool moves that reminder into **Outbox\Cancelled**.
- **Mistakes in the sheet are flagged, not guessed.** If a row is missing something, like a due date, that row is listed at the bottom of the chase list so you can fix it.
- **Nothing is sent without you.** You read every reminder before it goes out.

## What it replaces

A shop with about 40 credit invoices a month spends around **3 hours a week** going through its books, working out who is late and typing messages. This tool does that work in seconds. It also stops overdue invoices from being forgotten, and a forgotten invoice is money the shop never collects.
